Four ways to get sharper

Adaptive IQ test

Questions get harder or easier based on your answers. You get an IQ with a margin of error and a profile across four abilities.

Daily puzzle

Five new questions every day, the same for everyone. Build a streak and see how you compare.

Challenge a friend

Send a link. You both get the same twelve questions, and the highest IQ wins.

Training in levels

Ten levels per ability. Climb up and unlock harder questions at your own pace.
